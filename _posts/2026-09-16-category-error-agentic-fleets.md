Every safety mechanism that has ever proven effective in production software
decided a question the system could actually answer. Agentic fleets, by
contrast, stake their security on a predicate the system structurally cannot
evaluate — and dress the gap up as an engineering flaw. This is the category
error at the heart of the architecture: treating an underdetermined question of
intent[^undec] as an engineering problem to be solved with more code.

## The one lens

> Everything that has ever worked gated a decidable predicate. Everything that
> fails at fleet scale needs to gate an undecidable one — and dresses it up as
> an engineering gap instead of a category error.
